Harbor gateway outage

The first sustained customer-impact signal preceded the gateway rollout by at least 71 seconds.[S-08:881–889] The deploy controller began rollout at 09:13:52 UTC, and revision 7c14d2a first appeared in gateway telemetry at 09:14:09 UTC.[S-11:1432] [S-02:4–8] [S-08:905]

The rollout therefore did not initiate the incident. It did, however, overlap the rising-error interval and complicated recovery by changing connection-pool behavior while upstream failures were increasing.[S-08:905–974] [S-14:77–104]

The vendor outage amplified impact after 09:18:44 UTC, but available evidence does not establish that it caused the initial latency event.[S-14:77] [C-07] This finding remains qualified because vendor-side request traces are incomplete for 09:10–09:16 UTC.

Recommended follow-up: obtain the missing vendor traces, preserve deploy-controller clock calibration, and move the gateway rollback threshold into the release runbook.

Deliberately bounded

A source-grounded reasoning surface, not an autonomous truth machine.

Investigation Desk helps people and agents organize evidence, test claims, and preserve review decisions. It does not silently promote generated prose into accepted findings.
