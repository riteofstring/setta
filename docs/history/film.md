# Six years of Setta: the retrospective film

[Watch the film](https://setta.dev/history/#film) · [Illustrated history](README.md) · [Chronological feature history](feature-history.md)

**4:37.3 · 70 feature shots · 12 dated chapters · original 90 BPM score**

The November 23, 2026 cut follows ABCDE, ioVuu and Runcoder through the Setta
workbench, the separate Setta 3 and Setta 4 experiments, and the current React
library. Music accompanies on-screen feature captions; there is no spoken
narration. The tables below transcribe those captions and their source labels
in order. Times are rounded down to the start of the second.

Original recordings, restored builds, prepared data and built-in demonstrations
are identified separately. Historical build dates describe the footage, not
the first implementation of every feature. The full source history covers
capabilities that this selected visual tour does not demonstrate.

[Four focused demonstrations](feature-demos.md) add longer walkthroughs of Experiment Lens,
Review, Bench and RunPlan alongside this film. They are separate clips; the overview
and its chapter times remain unchanged.

The film opens at 0:00 with “Six years of ideas.”

## 4:24 — Applications built with it

The closing gallery shows the ambient instrument, Print Studio, Gaussian splat
studio and photo reconstruction. Their music, rendering and machine-learning
algorithms belong to the applications; Setta provides workspace interactions
and worker coordination. [The library-era gallery](features/libraries-and-detours.md)
records their sources. Houseplant is a capture by [Marcel Padilla](https://marcelpadilla.com/Gaussian_Splat_Object_Dataset/),
licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The ending reads “Now it’s a library.” and points to `@riteofstring/setta` and
the full history. The original score uses electric piano, upright bass, swung
drums and tape texture; the summer chapters have a distinct half-time groove.

## What the summer captures establish

The Setta 3 workspace and Easy Button use actual local Qwen3-30B-A3B inference.
The selected Easy Button request is in progress. The semantic graph is authored
research data with synthetic experiment results; the workflow is an edited,
validated draft that was not run. Neither demonstrates automatic cartography
or completed workflow execution.

Setta 4’s graph and report come from its original built-in demonstrations. The
restored live Director did not dispatch work successfully. These shots establish
the graph and artifact interfaces, not live orchestration or an agent-authored
report. [Image provenance](images.md#summer-2026-restoration-captures) records
exact source revisions and frame selections.

The selected footage does not establish remote SSH workspaces, dictation,
Python Canvas chat, linked-chat access edges or successful AI app-spec
generation. Their implementation history remains in the chronological guide.
