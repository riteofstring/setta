Product walkthrough

Three views over one Workspace document.

Build, Operate, and Record are saved projections of one WorkspaceHost. Runbook contributes domain sections and execution state; Workspace supplies the shell, canvas, panes, ports, edges, Inspector, views, controls, and managed windows.

Deliberately bounded

An operational control surface, not another orchestration engine.

Runbook coordinates bounded local or adapter-backed steps and makes authority legible. Provider-specific scheduling, fleet management, and secret storage remain outside the app.

The operational lifecycle.

Section schemas, transition authority, resumable execution, approval receipts, artifact references, and sealed records.

Outside the app

Infrastructure stays replaceable.

Runbook can call adapters without becoming the owner of clusters, credentials, calendars, or enterprise policy systems.

No general workflow scheduler
No secret vault
No remote worker fleet
No incident-management replacement

The authority boundary stays as visible as the work.
